Display color test

Manually cycle through black, white, red, green, blue, and gray. Look for dots that stay bright, dark, or colored. Colors do not change on their own.

How to test
  1. Move the browser to the display you want to check. Clean the screen first and set a comfortable brightness.
  2. Check one color at a time. Choose Next color or use the arrow keys. Press H to hide or restore the controls. Move the pointer to the upper right or press Esc to exit.
  3. If a spot persists across colors, inspect it with your eyes and compare an external photo. A screenshot usually cannot capture a physical panel defect. This tool does not automatically detect or certify bad pixels.

Observations

    Positions are relative to the test area, not physical pixel coordinates. They stay on this page and reset on refresh.
